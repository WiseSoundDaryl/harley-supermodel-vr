# Windows beta status

**The first Windows beta was released on November 20, 2026.** [Download the Windows player](https://github.com/WiseSoundDaryl/harley-supermodel-vr/releases/download/v0.1.0-beta.1/HarleyVR-Windows-x64.zip) or visit the [release page](https://github.com/WiseSoundDaryl/harley-supermodel-vr/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.1) for the two emulator source archives, launcher source and checksums.

| Headset | Edition | Playtest evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PSVR2 with Sony's official PC adapter | SteamVR / OpenXR | The player reported smooth long runs in both Quality and Performance on the reference PC at a configured 120 Hz. |
| Rift S with Touch controllers | Direct Meta OpenXR | The player reported smooth 80 Hz riding and confirmed controller vibration on vehicle and wall impacts as well as hard landings. |

PSVR2 includes the startup and in-game ride menu, camera and comfort adjustments, graphics presets and the optional startup Dream Ride. The Rift S edition preserves the original arcade interface and its tested rendering and haptic behavior. Its launcher temporarily disables Meta Asynchronous Spacewarp and restores the recorded setting on exit.

Both edition-specific source archives reproduced their reference executables byte-for-byte. Both player routes booted from fresh extractions with factory settings. The PSVR2 automated checks cover individual rider and transmission selections.

Both editions use genuine stereo riding, independent headset tracking and flat theater presentation for the game's flat screens. Performance depends on hardware, runtime and scene. Other PC headsets, macOS, Linux and standalone installations have no verified playtest for this beta.
